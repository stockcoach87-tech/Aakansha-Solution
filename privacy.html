Privacy Policy.

How information may be handled when you interact with the S R TRADERS website.

Effective date: 14 September 2026

1. Introduction

S R TRADERS respects the privacy of visitors and customers. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you visit this website or contact the business through information provided on the website.

2. Information We May Collect

If you voluntarily contact us or use the enquiry form, information may include your name, mobile number, email address, selected service, preferred contact method and the contents of your message.

You should only provide information that is relevant to your enquiry. Please do not submit passwords, payment credentials, bank account information, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive information through a general enquiry form unless specifically requested through an appropriate secure process.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information provided by you may be used to respond to enquiries, understand your service requirements, communicate about your request, provide customer assistance and maintain records reasonably necessary for business communication.

4. Data Protection

S R TRADERS seeks to handle personal information responsibly and to use reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the information and the website environment. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Third-Party Services

This static website does not by itself require a third-party analytics, payment or customer-management service. If third-party services are added in the future, they may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. Relevant service providers should be reviewed before submitting information through their systems.

6. Cookies and Analytics

This version of the website does not intentionally deploy advertising cookies or third-party analytics scripts. If analytics or similar technologies are added in the future, this section should be updated to describe the technology, purpose and applicable user choices.

7. Information Sharing

Information may be shared where necessary to respond to a customer request, comply with applicable law, protect rights or safety, or work with a relevant service provider. S R TRADERS does not represent that information supplied through a public website form should be treated as confidential professional or financial advice.

8. User Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to access, correction, withdrawal of consent, deletion or other handling of your personal information. To raise a privacy-related request, contact S R TRADERS using the contact details below.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for general business communication and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children through this website.

10. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, business practices or applicable requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
