CLAIM JOURNEY

A clearer path through the process.

Claim procedures vary by insurer and policy. The following is general guidance and does not guarantee approval, payment or a particular processing time.

!

Notify

Notify the applicable insurer through the prescribed channel as soon as reasonably possible and follow the policy's notification requirements.

02
□

Submit Documents

Gather and submit documents requested by the applicable insurer or claims administrator.

03
⌁

Claim Review

The applicable insurer reviews the claim according to the relevant policy terms, conditions and procedures.

04
✓

Track Status

Use the applicable insurer's claim tracking process and contact us for assistance with service-related enquiries where appropriate.

DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Be prepared with the right information.

Exact requirements depend on the claim type, policy and insurer. Depending on the situation, an insurer may request items such as:

✓ Policy or policy reference details
✓ Claim notification information
✓ Identity or contact documentation, where applicable
✓ Incident-related documents or records
✓ Bills, reports or supporting evidence, where applicable
✓ Bank/payment information if requested through the insurer's prescribed process
